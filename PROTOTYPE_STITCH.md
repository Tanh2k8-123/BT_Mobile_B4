## Three screens

1. **Student list** — `d66955bbea9541259372cd31504b4249`
   - Search, student rows with avatars, add action, and local-storage note.
2. **Student details** — `f79911fa46c44f7698695788fab825e0`
   - Full name, student ID, email, avatar, edit action, and delete-confirmation state.
3. **Add / Edit student** — `1ec72f1e37654a508c466f974f304814`
   - Shared create/edit form, device-photo or image-URL source, and edit-confirmation state.

Settings and confirmation prompts are modal overlays so the app keeps exactly three navigation screens. The working application adds an English/Vietnamese language choice in Settings and persists it on the device.
